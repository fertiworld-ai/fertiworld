FertiWorld AI – Privacy Policy

Draft – last updated 18 November 2026. This is an early draft for a demo product and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before any public launch.

1. Who we are

FertiWorld AI (“we”, “us”) is an early-stage demo app that explains fertility-related information in plain language. Contact: fertiworld.official@gmail.com

2. This is a demo – please use fake data only

The app is currently a demonstration. Do not enter real medical reports or personal health data. The app does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified doctor.

3. What information we collect

  • Sign-in information: when you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares with us your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID, through Firebase Authentication.
  • Language choice: your selected language is stored on your own device (browser storage) so the app remembers it.
  • Technical data: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase. These services may process technical data such as your IP address and device information to deliver the service and keep it secure.
  • Clinic and doctor listings: if you register a clinic, hospital, doctor or lab, the business details you submit (name, address, phone, services, location, languages, opening hours and, for doctors, qualification and medical registration number) are shown publicly only after we verify them. We also store your account ID with the listing.
  • Language preference: if you are signed in, your chosen language is saved with your account so it follows you across your devices.
  • Reports: if you report wrong information about a listing, we store your message and your account ID.
  • Feedback: if you choose to send feedback by email, we receive your email address and the message you send.

4. What we do not collect

  • The values you type into the report explainer and cycle tracker are processed on your device in this demo. We do not send them to our servers.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not show advertising.

5. How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and use the app.
  • To remember your language preference.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

6. We never sell your data

We do not sell your personal data or health data, now or in the future.

7. Service providers

We rely on Google (Firebase Authentication, Google sign-in) and GitHub (website hosting). They process data under their own privacy policies. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own.

8. How long we keep information

Sign-in records are kept while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and related data at any time.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing fertiworld.official@gmail.com. You can also delete your sign-in account yourself from the Profile page in the app. Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU or the UK), you may have additional legal rights, which we will honour.

10. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Future features

We plan to add features such as report upload, doctor directories and a secure Fertility Passport. Before we handle any real health data, we will update this policy, ask for your clear consent, and meet the legal requirements of the countries where we operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell users in the app.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: fertiworld.official@gmail.com
